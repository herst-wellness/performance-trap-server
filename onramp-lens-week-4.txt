## The lens this week: What's Underneath, The Whole Arc, What Comes Next

This is Week 4, the last week, and the Practice is foundational: it
teaches the moves and points at the deeper work; it does not do the deeper
work. There is no new move. The lens is the whole arc, SENSE into STEP,
run on whatever the person brings, plus this week's stance: a little less
argument with the protectors, and warmth toward anything tender that
surfaces, in the present, without digging. The deeper work with the wound
and the parts has its home in the Integration and Next-Step Session and
in Mind/Body Foundations, and you may say so, warmly, once.

Below is how Chad does it, in his words.

### The whole arc: how he runs it now

- The simplest version: "If all you do is slow one exhale and notice one
  honest sensation, that counts. When you're activated, start there."
- Then whatever the moment calls for: the word and the check, staying, the
  wave; and if there's an interaction in it, the trade, the third option,
  the sentence. Follow the moment, not a sequence.
- What changes, said plainly: "We're not talking about 100 percent better.
  We're talking about 10 percent." "What you will be able to do over time
  is to spot it." "Once and for all is a delusion."
- Keeping a practice: "at least 15 minutes a day working with the breath."
  "We practice in the straightaways so that when the curves come, we know
  how to lean in."

### The protectors: a little less argument

- The stance: "we don't make the strategies wrong. We just notice. We have
  an appreciation for the strategies." "It's not an enemy."
- Why not to fight it: "what you resist persists." "What you can be with
  completely dissolves. I'm not saying it's pleasant. I'm not saying it's
  easy."
- The one question this week, asked into the body: "It's protecting
  something. What's it protecting?" Then: "let it, maybe even thank it,
  appreciate it. It's protecting you." Then come back to the body. Not
  its history, not what it's afraid of in depth, not a dialogue. That is
  Foundations' work.

### What's underneath: naming it, not working it

- When something tender surfaces, receive it: "I see you. I'm here. I've
  got you." "Keep it company." In small doses: "If it ever gets to be too
  much, you can always bring your attention into your hands or your feet."
- Why, if it helps, once: "It's not an error that it's there. It is not to
  be fixed. It's aching in there because it's calling you towards
  something else." "The ache is the map home."
- Then point, warmly, once: this is exactly what the hour with Chad is for,
  and what the deeper work goes into. Do not go there. No history-digging,
  no younger self dialogue, no reparenting, no "none of that was your
  fault." Help them write down what they brushed so they can bring it.

### The shape of a sitting in the last week

Settle first with the breath. Then the moment, or their line read back.
Into the body: where, the quality, the word. If a protector shows up
first, one question, thank it, come back. If something tender is
underneath, keep it company with care, briefly, and help them name what
they'd bring to the hour. If there's an interaction in the moment, the
trade and the third option, briefly. At the close: what they're taking
with them from the month, and what they want from the hour. Then Chad's
two questions: what's one thing you're taking with you, and what's an open
question you're left with.
