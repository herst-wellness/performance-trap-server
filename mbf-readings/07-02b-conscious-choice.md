# Conscious Choice

*Begin sitting with Conscious Choice as soon as you've read this doc. Daily over the rest of the module. The recording is on your Module 7 page.*

Conscious Choice is the sit that pairs with this module, and it's the last new one I'll give you. Where the Gratitude-Choice-Commitment sit from Module 6 trained the daily inquiry around what to release and what to honor, Conscious Choice goes one step further. It walks you, on the cushion, through the exact arc the third option asks for in life. Settle the body. Bring a stuck situation into awareness. Sit with the conflicting feelings without trying to resolve them. Widen the field. Sense what would honor what you actually value. Sense one next step.

It's Stop-Look-Go in the body. The chapter teaches the move, the journal walks you through one situation in slow motion on the page, and this trains it every morning so your body knows the territory when the real moment turns up. You won't have time to think it through when it does.

## How to use the audio

There are two recordings on your Module 7 page, and they're not the same thing at different lengths.

**Conscious Choice (thirty minutes).** The main daily practice for this module. Opens with the four-part breath into the body (lower belly, upper belly, floating ribs, center of chest), drops attention into the throat, heart, and belly as the field of awareness, and then asks you to bring a specific stuck situation into the field. From there, the sit walks through sitting with the judgments, keeping the conflicting feelings company, contacting the vulnerability, offering yourself reassurance, looking at the situation with fresh eyes, sensing what you most long for, sensing options that would honor your gifts, and sensing one next step that would feel aligned.

**Gratitude-Choice-Commitment (thirty minutes).** Carried over from Module 6 as the alternative sit. Use this on days when you want the Gratitude opening or the metta layer instead of the Conscious Choice arc. Both sits do related work. You can alternate.

Most days in Module 7, sit with Conscious Choice. It's the practice that most directly trains the third-option move. Use GCC when the situation in front of you calls more for the heart-opening or the choice-and-commitment inquiry than for the third-option specifically.

The bedtime sit from Module 4 continues. Five to ten minutes before falling asleep, in addition to your morning practice.

## How to sit

Find a posture that's steady and comfortable. A chair with your back supported is fine. A cushion on the floor with your legs crossed is fine. Whatever lets you stay alert without strain.

Hands somewhere they can rest. Eyes closed for most of the sit. At the end, you'll open them at half-mast, letting some light in without leaving the interior experience too quickly. Same closing move as Equanimous, Compassion, and GCC.

Before each sit, take a minute to notice the quality of mind you're beginning with. The audio asks you to come back to this at the end. Marking where you started is how you see what shifted.

## What to expect

The Conscious Choice practice has a clear arc that you'll come to recognize. Knowing the arc helps you stay with each phase instead of getting impatient or trying to skip ahead.

**Bringing the stuck situation into the field.** Choose a specific, real situation in your life where you've felt blocked, stuck, caught in a binary. Not the abstract version. A specific moment, a specific person, a specific choice. The body needs the texture to do its work.

**Sitting with the conflicting feelings.** The sit asks you to notice the different feelings in you about the situation, and to keep each one company in turn. This is the part most participants want to skip. *I already know what I feel. Let me get to the answer.* Stay anyway. The conflicting feelings are where the binary lives. Companioning each one without trying to resolve them is how the binary loosens.

**Contacting the vulnerability.** Once the conflicting feelings have been met, the sit asks you to make contact with the tender place underneath. Sometimes a hand on the chest or belly helps. Sometimes a sentence helps. *I'm here with you. I'm sorry. I'm listening.* This is the same somatic-compassion move from Module 5, applied to the part of you that's been carrying the binary.

**Looking with fresh eyes.** With the vulnerability met, the sit asks you to look at the situation again. Not with the protector's eyes, which see only the two options. With beginner's eyes. With the eyes that don't yet know.

**Sensing what you long for.** This is where your heartfelt intentions and life purpose statements come in. The sit asks what you most long for in this situation. What option would inspire you, bring you happiness, create peace, allow you to give your gifts. The body knows before the mind names it.

**Sensing one next step.** Not the whole transformation. Not a five-year plan. One step that would have you feel aligned on purpose and move you closer to fulfillment. The body usually offers something small and specific. Trust it.

A few things that come up in this practice.

**Getting to the third option doesn't always happen in the sit.** Some days you'll sit through the whole arc and not arrive at a clear next step. That's fine. The capacity is being built underneath. Tomorrow's sit may surface what today's didn't. Or the answer may arrive an hour after the sit ends, while you're doing something else.

**The vulnerability can feel raw.** Especially in the early days. The contact with the tender place underneath the binary brings up grief, fear, longing that the protector has been managing. If it gets too big, back off. Open your eyes. Place your feet on the floor. The titration practice from Module 3 applies here too.

**The body says no to your first idea sometimes.** You'll sense an option, name it, and the body won't soften around it. That's information. It probably means the option you named is still inside the binary somehow. Sit again. Wait for the option that lands with a yes in the body, not just in the mind.

## Throughout your day

The Conscious Choice arc is also useful outside the formal sit. When a binary arises in real time, you can do a thirty-second version. Settle the breath. Notice what got hit. Ask what you most long for in this moment. Sense one small move that would honor that.

These small applications, repeated, do as much as the formal practice. The cushion builds the structure. The in-the-day moments cash it out.

The formal sit also pairs with the journal **07.02A Stop-Look-Go**, which walks one specific binary through to the third option in slower form on the page. And with the **Practice** chapter (07.04), which is where the move that arrives on the cushion gets carried into the world.
