# The Framework

*One page. The whole spine of the program. Photograph it for your phone, fold it into your wallet, tape it inside a drawer. Come back when the language fades.*

\

## SENSE: the inner half

The capacity to come back to yourself in the body. Built across Modules 1 through 5.

**S: Slow the breath.** Module 1. The straw breath. Five in, seven out. Send the nervous system the signal that it's safe to downshift.

**E: Enter the body.** Module 2. The U-turn. Leave the story. Go to where the feeling actually lives.

**N: Name what arises.** Module 2. Step back an inch. Describe what's there with specificity, from a small distance.

**S: Stay with what arises.** Module 3. Be With. Stay with the body's signal at the moment it fires, before the reaction takes over. The interruption move.

**E: Equanimity.** Modules 4 and 5. Two halves of the same letter. The *capacity* to stay through the whole wave without bailing (M4). The *compassion* that lets you meet what's there once you've stayed (M5).

\

## STEP: the outer half

The action that becomes possible once the contact is there. Built across Modules 6 and 7.

**S: Still.** Module 6. The half-second pause before the autopilot fires. Not a state you achieve, a move you make. One breath, in real time, while the room is still moving.

**T: Tune in to the Trade.** Module 6. Recognize, in the body, the moment you're about to trade your truth for the contract's promise. The throat clamp. The shoulder rise. The breath caught.

**E: Expand options.** Module 7. Widen the field when the bind has narrowed it. Story versus fact. The third option, the one the binary said wasn't available.

**P: Practice.** Module 7. The small honest move that breaks the old contract. The sentence said. The pause taken. The conversation you had instead of postponing.

\

## The thesis

**Contact first, then choice.**

Action without contact is more performance. Contact without action stays internal. The arc is both. SENSE is the contact. STEP is the choice.

\

## How to use this page

When you forget what you've learned, you'll forget specific letters or specific moves. You won't usually forget the shape. Come back here, find the letter you can't remember, and let the one-line definition bring the chapter back.

The framework isn't the practice. The practice is the practice. The framework is the map.
