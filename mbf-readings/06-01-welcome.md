# Welcome

The inner half of the program is done. What you built across Modules 1 through 5 is a body that can be trusted as a source of truth, a witness that can stay through the wave, and a heart that can meet what's underneath with kindness. SENSE is complete.

Module 6 is the doorway from that work into what becomes possible next.

We go from staying with what's tender to naming what's true.

## Where This Module Sits

\arcDiagram{6}

Modules 1 through 5 trained the capacity. Modules 6 through 8 train the action.

This is the pivot from SENSE to STEP. SENSE rebuilt the body as a trustworthy source of truth, the inner work of restoring contact with what got abandoned. STEP is what becomes possible once that contact is there: action, conversation, boundary, choice. The thesis of the whole program is contact first, then choice. Action without contact is more performance. Contact without action stays internal. The arc is both.

We're not stacking unrelated practices on top of each other. We're building a single capacity, in stages. Each module sets up the next.

## What Module 5 Set Up

In Module 5, you met the wound and the protector that grew up around it. You met them with compassion, the kind of contact the vulnerable part underneath had never quite received.

What we didn't fully name then was the other thing that happens when the protector softens. The wound stops being only a wound. It also becomes a source. The values you most care about, the ones that have been steering your choices from underneath without you realizing it, came from the places you got hurt. The qualities others rely on you for, the gifts you offer without trying, those started as adaptive strategies the protector built. The same energy that organized itself around protection has been organizing itself around contribution all along, usually with the dial turned up too high.

You don't invent your heartfelt intentions. You don't invent your life purpose. You uncover both. They've been pointing at you the whole time.

## What Module 6 Does

Three movements over the next few weeks.

We name what you most value, the inner half of the map. The values that grew up in the soil of your wound. The things your nervous system has been protecting and longing for since long before you had words for them. That's the work of **Heartfelt Intentions**.

We name what you uniquely offer, the outer half of the map. Not the strategies you've been performing, but the actual gifts underneath. The qualities others come to you for. The contribution that flows when you're not bracing. That's the work of **Life Purpose**.

Then we put both maps to use. With your intentions named and your purpose articulated, you have a felt sense of what's at stake in any given moment. You can finally start to recognize the trade as it's happening: the second your throat clamps, the moment your shoulders rise, the breath you catch when something asks you to abandon what matters for the sake of the contract's promise. That's the work of **Tune in to the Trade**.

## From STEP

In STEP, this module covers the first two letters: **S. Still** and **T. Tune in to the Trade.**

Still is the capacity that's been quietly building since Module 1. The breath, the witness, the equanimity, the compassion. All of it was making you steady enough to do what comes next. Module 6 names Still as the prerequisite explicitly. Without it, no tuning in is possible. The reactive nervous system can't see its own trades. It's too busy making them.

Tune in to the Trade is the move that recognizes, in the body, in real time, when you're about to trade your truth for the contract's promise. The recognition lives in the body first. A throat clamp. A shoulder tighten. A breath caught. The protector flares with its old reasoning. *You have to. They need this. You can't disappoint them.* That moment, the moment of the trade, is what this module trains you to see.

By the end of Module 6, you'll have the equipment to tune in. Module 7 (E. Expand Options, P. Practice) will give you what to do with what you've tuned in to.

## What You'll Notice Along the Way

A few things worth flagging upfront.

**The friend interviews can be vulnerable.** Asking three people to tell you what they see in you is not a small ask. Some participants flinch from it. Some over-prepare to control what the friends might say. The right move is to send the questionnaire, schedule the call, and stay open. What you receive may not match how you see yourself. That mismatch is the point.

**The Heartfelt Intentions work may surface fresh material from Module 5.** The five stories pull up wound-memories the protector and compassion work has now made more accessible. If something big surfaces, slow down. The titration practice from earlier modules applies here too.

**Tune in to the Trade can feel uncomfortable.** Once you have your intentions and purpose named, you'll start catching trades in real time that used to be invisible. That's the practice working. It can also feel like a flood at first, every conversation suddenly transparent in a way it wasn't before. You don't have to act on every trade you notice. Noticing is the first move. Acting comes later, in Module 7.
