# What Comes Next

*Fifteen minutes. Read it before Session Prep, and before the ritual.*

This is the shortest reading in the program, and the most practical. Before the ritual, before our final session, I want to lay out what happens after Mind/Body Foundations ends. Plainly, while you can think about it with a clear head. Decisions made inside a goodbye get made by the part of us that's moved, not the part that knows what it needs.

## What's true at the end

Eight months of this work builds something real. You can slow the breath on purpose. You know your kids on the bus by name. You've sat with sensations you used to flinch from. You know your contract and which disguise it wears. None of that expires when the program does. It's yours.

Here's the other thing that's true, and I'd be doing you a disservice to leave it out: eight months surfaces more than it finishes. Most people end this program able to see their pattern clearly and still get caught by it at full speed. You can name the trade now. Naming it is not the same as declining it in the middle of a Tuesday meeting with your heart pounding. The seeing came first. The unlearning takes longer, and it doesn't run on a program schedule.

## The three paths

**On your own.** The daily sit, the framework card, the sangha you've just built. For some people this is the right next chapter, and I mean that without reservation. The work was designed to be carried without me.

**A rhythm of check-ins.** A session once a quarter, or at the turn of each season. Not active construction, but not carrying it alone either. A standing time where someone who knows your whole arc looks at where you are and tells you the truth.

**Ongoing work.** Sessions every two weeks, like now, but with no curriculum and no finish line. We work the specific material these months surfaced but didn't finish. The pattern that still catches you at speed. The conversations you can now start but not yet hold. The question of whether the place you're planted can grow what you're trying to grow. I stay available between sessions, the way I have been. This isn't Mind/Body Foundations part two. It's the same two people without the map, going where your actual life needs to go.

## How the decision gets made

Bring your leaning to our final session. Session Prep asks for it directly. I'll bring mine: an honest read of what looks finished in you and what doesn't. If either of the continuing paths interests you, I'll put the specific terms, including cost, in writing within a few days after our session. You decide from your kitchen table, not from inside the ending. Nothing is owed in the room.

## One warning

Watch for the contract in this choice. It has a version of each answer. The good student stays because staying might please me. The invulnerable one leaves to prove they don't need anything. Both are the old costume, worn to a new occasion. If you notice either one driving, pause, come back to the body, and ask what you actually want. That move is the whole program in miniature, and this decision is a fine place to practice it.

## Summary

- The foundation is real and it's yours, whichever way you go.
- Three paths: on your own, a check-in rhythm, ongoing work. None is more advanced than the others.
- The decision happens after the ending, in writing, with clear eyes. Not in the emotion of the last session.
